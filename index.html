How to Play Dots & Boxes (1v1)

  • 1 Players take turns connecting two adjacent dots horizontally or vertically with a single line.
  • 2 Whoever draws the 4th and final line of a 1×1 square claims that box, fills it with their transparent gradient color, and scores +1 Box.
  • 3 Bonus Turn Rule: Completing one or two boxes grants you an immediate extra turn!
  • 4 When all dots are connected and every square is claimed, the player with the most boxes wins!
